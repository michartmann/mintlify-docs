## Our API Products

### Open Finance APIs for TPPs
Access comprehensive banking services including account information, payment initiation, and product data through our TPP APIs compliant with CBUAE Open Finance standards.

### Open Finance APIs for LFIs
Licensed Financial Institutions can leverage our specialized APIs for consent management, data sharing, and service initiation with enhanced security features.

### Customer Data Platform (CDP)
Integrate with our Customer Data Platform for advanced analytics, customer insights, and personalized banking experiences.
